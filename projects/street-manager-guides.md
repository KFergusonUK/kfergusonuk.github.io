A series of step-by-step video guides to Street Manager processes, presented
by an AI avatar of me.

## Why

Street Manager is the system most people in street works use daily, and the
gap between "the documentation exists" and "I know how to do this" is where
most of the friction lives. Written guidance is fine for reference, but
someone learning a process for the first time — or coming back to one they
do twice a year — is usually better served by watching it done.
