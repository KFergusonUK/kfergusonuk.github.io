*Working title. Unity and C#. Unfinished, and built for the fun of it.*

A 2.5D action game inspired by the Mega Drive Strike series — Desert
Strike, Jungle Strike, Urban Strike. Those games got a lot out of a fixed
camera angle and a small set of verbs, and I wanted to see how that holds
up when you rebuild it in a modern engine.
