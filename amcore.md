## Architecture

Full-stack system with a swappable AI backend, soft deletes throughout,
audit logging, and a workflow engine.

The AI layer is deliberately three-tier — worth explaining here, because
"we put AI in it" is the least interesting possible description of a system
where the safety design is the actual engineering.
