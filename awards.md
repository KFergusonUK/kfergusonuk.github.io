## GeoPlace Exemplar Awards — Platinum, five consecutive years

Durham County Council has held **Platinum standard for Street Gazetteer
maintenance every year since 2022**, awarded by GeoPlace for the quality and
maintenance of street data.

| Year | Award |
|------|-------|
| 2026 | Platinum — Street Gazetteer |
| 2025 | Platinum — Street Gazetteer |
| 2024 | Platinum Exemplar — Street Data |
| 2023 | Platinum Exemplar — Street Data |
| 2022 | Platinum Exemplar — Street Data, plus Highly Commended for street data maintenance and application |
