# Kevin Ferguson

**iHAMS Project Manager, Durham County Council**
**Technical Project Lead, GeoPlace**
Highways data · national standards · applied AI

I work on the systems and standards that sit underneath how roadworks are
planned, permitted and recorded in the UK — and I build software in the
same space.

I'm currently iHAMS Project Manager at Durham County Council and Technical
Project Lead at GeoPlace, with a substantive post as Road & Street Works
Systems Supervisor. Alongside that I'm Vice Chair of JAG(UK), Chair of
NEJAG, Co-Chair of NEHAUC, a member of the NUAR Senior Stakeholder Advisory
Group, and part of the current Street Works Code of Practice review.

## Selected work

### [StreetWorks SDK](/projects/streetworks-sdk/)
A Python SDK that normalises roadworks and street works data from over 130
official sources into a single consistent model.
`pip install streetworks`

### [AMCORE](/projects/amcore/)
An AI-native asset management and condition reporting engine for highways —
FastAPI and React, with a swappable AI backend and a three-tier safety layer.

### [Advanced AI Rights & Responsibilities Charter](/projects/ai-charter/)
A governance framework for advanced AI systems, archived with permanent DOIs
on Zenodo and OSF and listed on PhilPapers.

### [Shadowband](/projects/shadowband/)
An Android app built and shipped solo — approaching 7,000 downloads with a
4.2 average rating.
